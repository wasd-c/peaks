## Summary

Peaks 0.4.0 brings a redesigned account picker, unified Riot profiles and clearer live matches, with English, French and Korean support.

## New features

- Account nicknames and custom VALORANT agent or League champion icons, with a compact artwork picker.
- Language selection during onboarding, on the lock screen and in Settings.
- A changelog after updates, also available from Settings.

## Bug fixes

- Discord Rich Presence keeps its elapsed time when match statistics change.
- Refresh Riot sign-in opens a fresh sign-in instead of silently reusing the saved session.
- Account nicknames and chosen icons are preserved during session renewal.
- Smooth account hover transitions and a centered Settings button.

## Changes

- Accounts now focus on selection, with compact editing and animated sign-in feedback.
- Riot profiles and the current-match header no longer favor one game.
- VALORANT rosters adapt to the window to keep all ten agents visible, with team-colored scores.
- The initial language follows the device, with English as the fallback.
- Updated dependencies and stricter filtering of technical diagnostics.

## Removed

- Account summary tiles and redundant live-match labels.

## Known Issues / Problèmes connus

- **Enabling MFA directly from Peaks is not yet functional.** Configure MFA through [your Riot account](https://account.riotgames.com/) for now.
- **L’activation de la MFA directement depuis Peaks n’est pas encore fonctionnelle.** Configurez-la depuis [votre compte Riot](https://account.riotgames.com/) en attendant.

## Windows update

Update from the Peaks sidebar, or install **Peaks-Setup-0.4.0-x64.exe**. Saved accounts and settings are preserved.
